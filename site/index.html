Features

Everything you need,
nothing you don't.

Stable WebSocket Tunnel

Persistent WebSocket connection keeps your site online. Low-latency bidirectional tunnel for all file types.

Auto-Reconnect

Network dropped? SelfHost automatically reconnects when your connection comes back. No manual intervention needed.

Binary File Support

Serve images, fonts, PDFs, and any binary asset — not just HTML and CSS. Full static site support out of the box.

Real-Time Analytics

Live visitor counts, daily, monthly, and all-time stats — displayed right in the app. No external service required.

No Account Required

No sign-up, no OAuth, no email. Just install the app, pick a folder, and go. Free tier works immediately on launch.

Foreground Service

Runs as an Android foreground service. Your site stays live even when the screen is off or you switch apps.
